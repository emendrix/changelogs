Section 1 now opens with a statement that the manufacturer draws up the declaration of performance and determines the product-type under the listed systems, and it adds a new System 1.6 covering construction products for which a European Technical Assessment has been issued, describing how notified bodies and manufacturers are to treat that assessment and which tasks they need not undertake. [Annex V, v1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32011R0305#anx_V) [Annex V, v2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02011R0305-20140616#anx_V)

Within systems 1+, 1, 2+, 3 and 4, the wording describing the manufacturer's and notified body's tasks is reworded, replacing references to type testing, type calculation and issuing certificates with references to assessments and verifications on which the notified body decides whether to issue, restrict, suspend or withdraw the certificate, and referring to sample testing and audit-testing taking place at the manufacturing plant or storage facilities rather than simply 'at the factory'. [Annex V, v1](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32011R0305#anx_V) [Annex V, v2](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02011R0305-20140616#anx_V)

Section 2's definitions of the product certification body, factory production control certification body and testing laboratory are rewritten to describe them as bodies notified in accordance with Chapter VII rather than by competence and responsibility, the term 'testing laboratory' becomes 'laboratory', and section 3's heading gains the words 'Horizontal notifications' while item 4 changes from 'Noise absorption' to 'Acoustic performance'.
